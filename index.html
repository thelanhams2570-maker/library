About Anthology

Data syncs automatically to your private Supabase database and updates in real time across your devices. Export a JSON backup regularly from Data tools below as an extra safety net.

Your personal library stays private to your account. Book club meetings, RSVPs and ratings are visible to the other members of clubs you join. When you use an AI feature (recommendations, lookups), the book titles/authors involved are sent to Claude (Anthropic) to generate a response - no other personal data is included. Feedback you submit is readable only by the app owner.
